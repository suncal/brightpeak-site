What Facebook ads management includes

Facebook ads management means a specialist plans, launches and improves your paid campaigns on Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and the Audience Network) so you don't have to learn Ads Manager yourself. With BrightPeak Ads, monthly management covers:

  • Campaign optimization: budgets, bids, audiences and placements reviewed and adjusted every week.
  • Creative testing: new ad angles, hooks and formats tested against your current winners.
  • Budget management: spend moved toward the ad sets that bring in leads or sales.
  • Tracking checks: the Meta Pixel and Conversions API stay healthy so the algorithm optimizes on real results.
  • Weekly updates and a monthly report: plain-English results, what changed and what's next.

Who it's for

Small businesses, local service companies and e-commerce brands that already know Facebook and Instagram can bring customers but don't have the time, or the in-house skills, to run ads well. You don't need a huge budget to start.

How we manage Meta ads

  1. Tracking first. Before spending more, we confirm the Pixel and Conversions API record the events that matter: leads, purchases, bookings.
  2. Simple structure. Fewer, better-funded campaigns give Meta's algorithm enough data to learn.
  3. Constant creative. Creative is the biggest lever on Meta today, so we keep testing new ads.
  4. Clear reporting. You always know what you spent and what it brought in.
