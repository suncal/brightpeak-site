Facebook ads management pricing, explained

Most agencies charge either a flat monthly retainer or a percentage of ad spend (commonly 10–20%), often with a setup fee on top. BrightPeak Ads uses fixed prices: an account audit from $150, setup with Pixel and Conversions API from $450, and monthly management from $950, with no long-term contract.

How agencies price Facebook ads management

ModelHow it worksWatch out for
Flat monthly retainerOne fixed fee per month regardless of spend.Make sure the scope (creative, reporting, calls) is written down.
Percentage of ad spendFee is a share of what you spend on Meta, often 10–20%.The agency earns more when you spend more, whether or not results improve.
HybridA base retainer plus a percentage above a spend threshold.Harder to predict month to month.
Performance-basedPaid per lead or sale.Rare, usually with strict lead definitions and minimums.

Setup fees, minimum ad budgets and 6–12 month contracts are also common. Ask about all three before you sign.

Frequently asked questions

How much do agencies charge for Facebook ads management?

Most charge a monthly retainer or a percentage of ad spend, commonly 10–20%. BrightPeak Ads charges a fixed fee from $950 per month.

Is a percentage of ad spend or a flat fee better?

A flat fee keeps the agency's pay independent of how much you spend, which makes budgeting easier and removes the incentive to push spend up.

Is there a setup fee?

Setup with Pixel and Conversions API is a separate one-time package from $450. If your account is already set up correctly, you may not need it.

What is the minimum ad budget?

There's no fixed minimum. We'll recommend a starting budget based on your goals and what a lead or sale is worth to you.

Can I start with just an audit?

Yes. The account audit from $150 gives you a ranked fix list you can act on yourself or with us.
