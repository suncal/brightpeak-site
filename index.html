About us

What is BrightPeak Ads?

BrightPeak Ads is a small, fully remote Meta ads service. We work over chat and email during US Eastern business hours. You keep ownership of your ad account, Pixel, and data, and give us partner access. You pay Meta directly for ad spend and pay us a fixed fee for the work.

Meta Pixel
A small piece of code on your website that tells Meta when someone views a page, adds to cart, or buys. Meta uses these events to report results and to find more people likely to act.
Conversions API
A server-to-server connection that sends the same events from your website or store directly to Meta. It keeps tracking working when browsers block the Pixel or people clear their cookies.
Event deduplication
Matching each event sent by both the Pixel and the Conversions API with a shared event ID, so Meta counts every purchase or lead once instead of twice.

How it works

How does working with BrightPeak Ads work?

Working with BrightPeak Ads takes four steps. You send your goals, we review your ad account and send a fixed quote, then we fix or build your setup, check tracking, and launch. After launch you get weekly updates and a monthly report in plain English. Work usually starts within a few business days.

  1. Send us your goals

    Tell us what you sell, who you sell to, and what a good result looks like for you, whether that is leads, booked calls, or online sales.

  2. Get a fixed quote

    We review your ad account and send a fixed quote that covers the full scope of work. No surprises later and no extra hourly bills.

  3. We set up and launch

    We fix or build your setup, check that the Pixel and Conversions API are tracking correctly, and launch campaigns built around your goals.

  4. Stay in the loop

    You get weekly updates and a monthly report in plain English, showing what we changed, what it cost, and what it brought in.

Everything is handled over chat and email, during US Eastern business hours. No long calls needed.

FAQ

What are the most common questions?

The most common questions are about budget, contracts, account ownership, what we need to start, working outside the US, and how fast we can begin. In short: no big budget or long contract is needed, you own everything, and work usually starts within a few business days.

Do I need a big budget?

No. Many small businesses start with a modest daily budget. We will suggest a realistic starting budget based on your goals and scale up only when the numbers support it.

Do you require a long contract?

No. Audits and setup are one-time projects. Monthly management is month to month, and you can stop at any time.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Your ad account, Business Manager, Pixel, and data stay yours. You give us partner access, and you can remove it whenever you like.

What do you need from me to start?

Your goals, partner access to your Business Manager and ad account, access to your website for tracking setup, and any brand assets or product photos you already have.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes. We work fully remotely, so we can work with businesses anywhere. Updates are sent during US Eastern business hours.

How fast can you start?

Usually within a few business days of agreeing on a quote and getting access. An audit is often the quickest first step.
