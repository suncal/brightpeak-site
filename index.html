FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a big budget?

No. Many small businesses start with a modest daily budget. We will suggest a realistic starting budget based on your goals and scale up only when the numbers support it.

Do you require a long contract?

No. Audits and setup are one-time projects. Monthly management is month to month, and you can stop at any time.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Your ad account, Business Manager, Pixel, and data stay yours. You give us partner access, and you can remove it whenever you like.

What do you need from me to start?

Your goals, partner access to your Business Manager and ad account, access to your website for tracking setup, and any brand assets or product photos you already have.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes. We work fully remotely, so we can work with businesses anywhere. Updates are sent during US Eastern business hours.

How fast can you start?

Usually within a few business days of agreeing on a quote and getting access. An audit is often the quickest first step.

Not sure where your ads stand?

Send us a message and we will take a look at your ad account, free, and tell you what we would fix first.
