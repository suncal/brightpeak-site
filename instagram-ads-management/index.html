Instagram ads management that pays its way

Instagram ads are bought and managed in Meta Ads Manager, the same place as Facebook ads. BrightPeak Ads manages Instagram Reels, Stories and feed ads alongside Facebook for a fixed fee from $950 per month, with creative testing, weekly updates and no long-term contract.

Instagram placements we run

  • Reels: short vertical video, usually the cheapest reach on Instagram.
  • Stories: full-screen ads between friends' stories, good for offers and retargeting.
  • Feed: image, video and carousel ads in the main feed.
  • Explore: ads shown to people browsing new content.

Why manage Instagram and Facebook together

Meta's algorithm finds results across placements. Running Instagram and Facebook from one campaign usually gives it more room to find cheaper conversions than splitting them. Where Instagram clearly performs better for your audience, we shift budget and creative toward it.

Creative made for Instagram

Vertical 9:16 video, a strong hook in the first seconds, captions for sound-off viewing, and user-generated-style content tend to work best. We test new angles continuously so ads don't wear out.

Frequently asked questions

Are Instagram ads managed separately from Facebook ads?

No. Both run in Meta Ads Manager, so one management package covers both.

How much does Instagram ads management cost?

Monthly management starts at $950 per month. Ad spend is separate and paid to Meta.

Do you make the ad creative?

We plan and test creative angles and can work with your photos, videos and brand assets. Tell us what you have and we'll suggest what's needed.

Do I need a business Instagram account?

Yes. A professional (business or creator) account connected to your Facebook Page and Business Manager.
